Small while watching, a square for the sale

While watching, the picture is a short, wide strip (about 3:1), so the window stays out of the way. When a sale comes in Till switches the picture to 1:1 and iOS reshapes the window into a square for the code; after Paid it shrinks back. Till chooses the shape, iOS the size. People pinch it to the size they like; the content adapts to the size it's given, edge to edge with no padding baked into the frames. The corners stay empty: iOS draws ✕ at the top left and ⤢ at the top right when the window is tapped.
